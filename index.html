About this reviewer

A study tool built for an Application Development class project.

Read first

Every subject has short notes with definitions, key points, and examples written for first-year students.

Then practice

Answer multiple-choice questions one at a time. Go back and change answers before you submit.

Learn from mistakes

After you submit, see your score and the correct answer with an explanation for every question.

Bring your own quizzes

Upload questions from a Word (.docx) file. They are checked, saved in the database, and shared with your class.

Private by design

The built-in subjects need no account, and scores stay in this browser tab. Accounts use a Gmail code, and passwords are stored only as secure hashes.
